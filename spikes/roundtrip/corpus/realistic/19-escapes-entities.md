# Escapes and entities

Literal characters: \*not emphasis\*, \_not either\_, \# not a heading,
1\. not a list, \[not a link\](url), \`not code\`, a backslash \ and a pipe \|.

Unnecessary escapes: \a \b \- \.

Entities: &copy; &amp; &lt;tag&gt; &nbsp; &#169; &#xA9; &quot;quoted&quot;.

Characters that need no escape here: 2 * 3 = 6, snake_case_name, a_b_c.

Underscores inside words: foo_bar_baz and __init__.py and file_name.md.

Asterisks: 5*6*7 and *a*b.

An ampersand & on its own and a < b > c.
